What is the minimum salary?

The campaign states a minimum monthly salary of RM3,000. The issuing bank's current eligibility criteria and income assessment apply.

Is the credit card really free?

The campaign advertises RM0 fee and no hidden charges. Always review the bank's official fee schedule for finance charges, late payment fees, service tax and any other applicable charges.

The campaign highlights IC and EPF information. The bank may request additional documents through its secure application process.

No. Every application is subject to the issuing bank's eligibility checks, credit assessment and final approval.

No. This landing page does not collect identity or financial documents. Use only the bank's authorised secure application channel.
